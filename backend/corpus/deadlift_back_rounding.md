# Deadlift: Lower Back Rounding

Lower back (lumbar) rounding under load, as opposed to a neutral or
slightly extended spine, is one of the more commonly flagged deadlift form
issues because it shifts stress from the hips and posterior chain onto
spinal flexion under load.

Contributing factors: starting the pull with the hips too low (turning it
into a squat-pull hybrid), bar drifting away from the shins, or insufficient
lat engagement to keep the bar close.

Fixes: cue "protect the chest" or "proud chest" at setup, drill the
hip-hinge pattern separately (Romanian deadlifts, kettlebell hinges) before
loading the full deadlift, and reduce load until the lifter can maintain a
neutral spine through the full range.
