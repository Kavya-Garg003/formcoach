# Squat: Excessive Forward Lean / Rounded Spine

Some forward torso lean is normal and mechanically necessary in a squat,
especially in a high-bar or front-loaded variation. The concern is excessive
lean paired with a rounding lower back, since that shifts load away from the
hips and onto a flexed spine.

Contributing factors: limited ankle dorsiflexion, weak upper back and core
bracing, or a bar/load path that sits too far forward.

Fixes: cue a hard exhale-and-brace before descending, work on ankle mobility
(heel-elevated squats can help temporarily), and consider a more upright
squat variation such as a front squat or goblet squat while the pattern is
being rebuilt.
