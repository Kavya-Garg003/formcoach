# Lunge: Insufficient Depth

Adequate lunge depth is typically described as the back knee lowering to
within a few inches of the floor with the front thigh approaching parallel
to the ground, though exact depth depends on limb length and mobility.

Shallow lunges are frequently a stability issue rather than a strength
issue -- the lifter stops short because balance feels precarious, not
because the muscles are maxed out. Holding onto a support (wall, rack) for
the first several sets while depth is rebuilt is a reasonable regression.
