# Squat: Insufficient Depth

A rep is generally considered to reach acceptable depth when the hip crease
drops to or below the top of the knee (parallel or below), though depth
targets vary by training goal and by individual hip anatomy.

Cutting depth short is often driven by one of: tight ankles (limited
dorsiflexion), tight hips, a mobility limitation in the upper back that
causes the lifter to bail out of the bottom position, or simply a habit of
stopping early because full depth feels unstable.

Checks: film from the side at roughly hip height and compare hip-crease
height to knee height at the bottom of the rep. A goblet-squat hold or a
box squat set just below target depth can help a lifter build confidence
and control in the bottom range before returning to full range under load.
